Why V7 needs this now

V7's audience grew fast as Egyptians looked for local alternatives to international soda brands. Much of that conversation happens on food pages, reels and comment sections that never tag @v7egypt — and none of it shows up in Meta Insights.

Today

  • Insights stop at V7's own pages and posts
  • Mentions on food pages and creators' reels go unseen
  • Complaints (availability, taste, price) surface late, by chance
  • No like-for-like view against Pepsi, Coca-Cola or Spiro Spathis

With V7 Pulse

  • Every public mention of V7 and each flavor, in one place
  • Sentiment and topics read in Egyptian dialect, not translated
  • Alerts within hours when complaints or volume spike
  • Weekly share of voice against the competitors you choose

The weekly report

Every Sunday morning the team receives a report built from the same data — readable on a phone, ready to forward to management.

  1. 01
    Analytics overviewMentions, engagement, views, sentiment, emotion, language and platform split, with week-over-week change.
  2. 02
    Key themesThe five conversations that mattered, each linked to the posts behind it.
  3. 03
    Competitor benchmarkShare of voice, views and engagement for V7 against the chosen brands.
  4. 04
    Positive and negative driversWhat people praise and what they complain about — taste, price, availability, packaging.
  5. 05
    Top posts and voicesHighest-reach posts and the creators and pages mentioning V7.
  6. 06
    Comment analysisWhat V7’s own followers ask and feel in the comments under brand posts.
  7. 07
    Keywords and hashtagsThe words and tags rising this week, in Arabic and English.
  8. 08
    RecommendationsThree to five concrete actions for content, community and sales teams.

What we need to start

Most of the work sits with us. From V7 we need a short list of inputs in the first week — about two hours of the marketing team’s time.

From V7

  • Brand and product keywordsHow customers write V7 and each flavor — Arabic, English and Franco (e.g. في سفن, “v seven”, كريم صودا).
  • Competitor listFive to eight brands to benchmark against, with their official pages.
  • Campaign calendar and hashtagsUpcoming launches and campaigns such as Summer of Colors, so their impact is measured separately.
  • Alert contactsWho gets urgent alerts, on which channel (WhatsApp or email), and what counts as urgent.
  • Meta Business read accessoptionalAdds follower growth and reach for V7’s own pages, which public data can’t show.
  • Sales or distribution dataoptionalWeekly figures by region let us connect online buzz to sales and stock-outs.
  • One point of contactA 30-minute review every two weeks to tune keywords and report content.

Handled by us

  • Data collectionDaily automated collection of public posts and comments through Apify, already tested on V7’s pages.
  • Arabic-first AI analysisA language model reads each post for relevance, sentiment, emotion and topic — filtering out unrelated posts that keyword search picks up.
  • Database and historyEvery post is stored, so trends build up month over month.
  • Private hostingDashboard on a dedicated server with HTTPS and a login for each team member.
  • Report deliveryWeekly report generated and sent automatically; alerts sent as they happen.
  • MaintenanceFixing collectors when platforms change, and tuning the AI to Egyptian slang as it evolves.

What to expect from the data

  • Public conversation only. Private profiles, closed groups and direct messages are not collected.
  • X is the least stable source. X restricts automated access more than other platforms; expect occasional gaps there.
  • Keyword search is noisy. In our test, one in three search results was truly about V7. The AI relevance check removes the rest before anything is counted.
  • No audience demographics from Facebook. Age and gender of commenters aren’t public; Meta Business access fills part of that gap for V7’s own pages.
