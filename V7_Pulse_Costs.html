Prices used

  • Claude: Opus 5 $5 / $25, Opus 5.5 $4 / $20, Sonnet 5 $2 / $10, Haiku 4.5 $1 / $5 per million input / output tokens. Cached input is billed at about a tenth of the input price, Batch API at half price, web search at $10 per 1,000 searches. Claude pricing ↗
  • Apify: Free $0 ($5 of usage a month), Starter $17 a month billed yearly ($19 of usage), Scale $179 a month billed yearly ($199 of usage). Usage above the plan’s amount is billed on top. Per-result prices from each scraper’s store page. Apify pricing ↗
  • Hostinger VPS: KVM 1 $6.49, KVM 2 $8.99, KVM 4 $12.99 a month at signup; renewal $11.99, $14.99 and $28.99. Check the price for a 12-month term at checkout. Hostinger VPS ↗
